# Data sources and credits

**Hotel list** (`data/catalog.json`) is built from the FHR and THC property files in
[borski/travel-hacking-toolkit](https://github.com/borski/travel-hacking-toolkit), MIT License,
Copyright (c) 2026 Michael Borohovski. Those files are generated from the community map kept by
US Card Guide (美卡指南).

**Prices** come from Google Hotels through SerpApi's Google Hotels Price Calendar API, under your own
SerpApi account and its terms.
